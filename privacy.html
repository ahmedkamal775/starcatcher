Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who we are

Starcatcher Adventures publishes short animated videos for children. "Starcatcher" is also the name of a private desktop tool used by the channel's creator to prepare and upload those videos. Contact: ahmed.kamal775@gmail.com.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not collect any personal information.

Children

Our videos are made for children. We do not collect personal information from children. Our YouTube videos are marked as made for kids, so YouTube limits data collection and turns off comments and personalised ads on them.

The Starcatcher app and Google user data

The Starcatcher app is used only by the channel's owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Google account. It uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and set their title, description, tags and "made for kids" setting;
  • read the owner's channel information and the status and statistics of the owner's own videos.

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. The app does not share Google user data with anyone, does not sell it, does not use it for advertising, and does not access other people's accounts or data. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

By using the app, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or through the Google security settings page. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes the app's access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
